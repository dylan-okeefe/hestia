# Escape Room Adventure Planning: CT vs VT

## Current Info
- **Location**: 888 Chesterfield Rd, Florence, MA
- **Travelers**: Timo, TJ (born 8/2/2016, ~8-9 years old), Alicia (ex-wife)
- **Goal**: Choose between CT or VT for escape room adventure

## Decision Framework

### 1. Location & Travel Time
- **Connecticut**:
  - Approx. 1.5-2 hours from Florence, MA to major hubs (Hartford, New Haven, Stamford)
  - Closer to Boston metro area, potentially more family-friendly options
- **Vermont**:
  - Approx. 2-2.5 hours to Burlington, Montpelier, or smaller towns
  - More scenic, but fewer options for younger kids; check specific venues

### 2. Age-Appropriateness (TJ is 8-9)
- Look for rooms rated for 8+ or designed for families
- Many escape rooms have age restrictions; verify with venues
- Consider difficulty levels and theme (some are too scary for younger kids)

### 3. Cost Comparison
- Check pricing per person (kids often get discounts)
- Group booking rates if possible
- Any seasonal promotions

### 4. Additional Activities
- **CT**: More urban options, museums, parks nearby
- **VT**: Scenic drives, outdoor activities, nature centers

### 5. Booking Logistics
- Availability for desired date/time
- Cancellation policies
- Group size limits

## Next Steps
1. Research specific venues in both states
2. Check age restrictions and difficulty levels
3. Estimate travel time and costs from Florence, MA
4. Consider combining with other activities
5. Set a deadline for decision (e.g., 2 weeks out)
